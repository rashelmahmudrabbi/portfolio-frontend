About

AI research with a practical mindset.

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering graduate with a strong academic record and a research focus on Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, and Medical Image Analysis.

My work explores how modern deep learning models can solve challenging visual problems while remaining interpretable, reliable, and useful in real-world settings.

I'm particularly interested in Explainable AI, multimodal learning, trustworthy AI, and resource-constrained healthcare systems, with the goal of pursuing advanced research and graduate study.

Research Interests

Questions I want to explore

My research interests center on building accurate, explainable, and trustworthy AI systems.

01

Medical Image Analysis

Deep learning methods for diagnosis, classification, segmentation, and clinical decision support from medical images.

02

Computer Vision

Vision architectures that learn robust representations for complex real-world image understanding tasks.

03

Explainable AI

Interpretable AI using techniques such as Grad-CAM, LIME, SHAP, and attribution-based analysis.

04

Trustworthy AI

Confidence-aware, uncertainty-aware, and transparent AI systems designed for responsible deployment.

05

Multimodal Learning

Combining visual and clinical information to build richer representations for intelligent healthcare systems.

06

Remote Sensing

Deep learning for satellite image classification, land-use analysis, and geospatial intelligence.
